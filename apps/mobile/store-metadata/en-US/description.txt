Give your pet the health monitoring they deserve.

Pet Health OS uses artificial intelligence to track, analyze, and protect your pet's wellbeing — every single day. Whether you have a dog, cat, rabbit, or bird, our app gives you the same level of insight that veterinarians use, right in the palm of your hand.

---

CORE FEATURES

AI Health Score
Every morning, Pet Health OS calculates a 0-to-100 health score for your pet based on the data you've logged. The score accounts for activity, appetite, behavior, sleep patterns, and more — giving you a single, easy-to-understand number that tells you how your pet is doing today compared to their own baseline.

Camera Health Scan
Point your camera at your pet and let GPT-4o Vision do the work. Our AI visually analyzes coat condition, eye clarity, posture, and body weight distribution in seconds. No special equipment needed — just your phone's camera. Supports dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds.

Anomaly Detection
Subtle changes are the earliest warning signs of illness. Pet Health OS continuously monitors your pet's health patterns and sends you a push alert the moment something unusual appears — a drop in appetite, unusual lethargy, or a change in bathroom habits. Catch problems before they become emergencies.

Health Trends (30-Day Tracking)
See the full picture with rolling 30-day trend charts. Track how your pet's health score evolves over time, identify seasonal patterns, and share detailed trend reports with your veterinarian at your next visit.

HOW IT WORKS

Step 1 — Log
Each day, spend 60 seconds answering a few simple questions about your pet: Did they eat well? How was their energy? Any changes in behavior? The more you log, the smarter the AI becomes.

Step 2 — Scan
Use the built-in camera scan to capture a quick visual snapshot of your pet. The AI checks for visible signs of discomfort, weight changes, or coat issues that can be easy to miss in daily life.

Step 3 — Get Your Score
Within seconds, Pet Health OS generates your pet's daily health score along with personalized insights and recommendations. If anything needs attention, you'll know immediately.
