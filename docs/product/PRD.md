# Product Requirements Document — Pet Health OS

_Version 1.0 | May 2025 | MVP Scope_

## Vision

> Give every pet owner a data-driven early warning system that catches health problems before their pet shows symptoms — the way a vet would, but every single day.

## Target Users

**Primary:** Dog and cat owners in the US, aged 25–45, who treat their pets as family members. They spend $1,000+ annually on pet care and are anxious about missing early health warning signs.

**Secondary:** Multi-pet households, elderly pet owners with limited mobility, new pet owners learning what "normal" looks like for their pet.

## Core Value Propositions

1. **Early detection** — Catch illness 48–72 hours before symptoms are obvious
2. **Daily intelligence** — "Your dog's health score today is 87/100" — a simple number that tells you everything
3. **Visual health check** — Take a photo, get an instant AI assessment
4. **Peace of mind** — Know when to call the vet, avoid unnecessary emergency visits

## Problem Statement

Pet owners have no systematic way to monitor their pet's health between vet visits. By the time symptoms are visible, illness has often progressed significantly. Vet visits are expensive ($150–$500+) and owners feel guilty for "not noticing sooner."

## Key User Stories

### Must Have (MVP)

- As a dog owner, I want to log my dog's daily health in under 2 minutes, so I can build a health history without it feeling like a chore
- As a cat owner, I want a daily health score that tells me at a glance if my cat is doing well, so I don't have to interpret raw data
- As a pet owner, I want to take a photo of my pet and get an instant health assessment, so I can check on visible symptoms without a vet visit
- As a pet owner, I want to receive an alert when my pet's behavior deviates significantly from their normal, so I can act before they show obvious symptoms
- As a Pro subscriber, I want these AI capabilities unlocked for multiple pets, so I can monitor my whole household

### Should Have (Beta)

- As a pet owner, I want to export a health report PDF to share with my vet, so appointments are more productive
- As a pet owner, I want to see a 90-day trend chart for each health metric, so I understand long-term patterns
- As a pet owner, I want to refer friends and get a free month of Pro, so I'm rewarded for sharing

### Nice to Have (Post-Launch)

- As a vet, I want to receive health reports directly from pet owners, so I'm better prepared for appointments
- As a multi-pet owner, I want a household health dashboard, so I can monitor all pets at once

## Monetization

**Free tier:**
- 1 pet
- Daily health logging
- Basic health score
- 3 camera analyses/day
- No anomaly alerts (score only)

**Pro tier ($9.99/mo or $79.99/yr, 14-day free trial):**
- Up to 10 pets
- Anomaly detection and push alerts
- 50 camera analyses/day
- Health report PDF export
- Priority support

## Non-Goals (MVP)

- Vet booking integration
- AI audio analysis (breathing, coughing)
- Social features (sharing with other owners)
- Multiple language support (US English only for MVP)
- Desktop web optimization (mobile-first)

## Constraints

- 14-day MVP timeline (1–3 person team)
- Built on Lovable (AI-assisted development)
- Must pass Apple App Store review
- No medical diagnoses (liability)
- GDPR compliant from day 1 (EU market potential)
